`rules`

$$
\begin{align}
(\vec{a} + \vec{b}) \text{ is a vector}\\
\vec{a} + \vec{b} &= \vec{b} + \vec{a} \\
(\vec{a} + \vec{b}) + \vec{c} &= \vec{a} + (\vec{b} + \vec{c})
\end{align}
$$

$$
\begin{align}
\vec{A} + \vec{0} = \vec{A}\\
\text{let} |\vec{0}| = b
\end{align}
$$
