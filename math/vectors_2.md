`scalars`
are treated as single numbers, represent physical quantities with magnitude only  <br>
speed = scalar (magnitude - rate of covered distance)  <br>
velocity = vector (magnitude and direction)  <br>

so vector = arrow (juxtaposition)  <br>

x is rotated by positive 90 degrees to form y axis in cartesian  <br>
in 3D space also is vector $\vec{A}$ $\sum$ of three vectors $a_1i + a_2j + a_3k$  <br>

coordinates = elements of sum of vectors  <br>

## length of 3D vector
$\vec(A)$ is hypotenuse of right triangle  <br>
$\vec{A} = \sqrt{a^{2}_{1}}$
• negativeness is taken care of by the sense

square root `$\sqrt$` is a multivalued function, although zero has only one square root
• each complex number greater than zero has two real square roots
